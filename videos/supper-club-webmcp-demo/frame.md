# Supper Club AI — Video Design Truth

## Concept angle

An animated host ledger: the real Supper Club workspace remains the evidence,
while precise editorial annotations reveal how people and WebMCP agents share
one plan.

## Canvas

- Size: 1920 × 1080
- Base: `#0d0d0b`
- Safe area: 72 px left/right, 54 px top, 90 px bottom
- Caption keep-out: bottom 150 px whenever product text occupies the lower edge

## Palette

- Night ink: `#0f0f0e`
- Night canvas: `#0d0d0b`
- Bone paper: `#eee6dd`
- Deep paper: `#e1d8cf`
- Signal red: `#983423`
- Bright signal: `#ef7458`
- Seed green: `#bac467`
- Dark seed: `#6d752c`
- Marginalia blue: `#aeb5e6`
- Muted warm gray: `#b8ad9d`

## Typography

- Editorial display: `Garamond`, 400/700
- Functional and data voice: `IBM Plex Mono`, 400/700
- Condensed chapter labels: `League Gothic`, 400
- Headlines: 72–118 px
- Scene labels: 24–30 px, uppercase, tracked
- Evidence labels: 22–28 px

## Composition

- The real recorded UI is the primary focal point.
- Crop or mask toward one proof point; never shrink the interface into an
  unreadable desktop thumbnail.
- Use thin ledger rules, corner registration marks, chapter numerals, and
  compact evidence labels as the secondary focal point.
- Tool and provider names use monospaced type.
- On-screen lists carry details that narration intentionally omits.

## Motion

- Entrances settle with `power3.out`; no bounce.
- Use fast editorial wipes and restrained scale changes at scene openings.
- Reveal evidence sequentially when narration reaches it.
- Hold proof frames still long enough to read; no ornamental breathing loops.
- Use signal red for action and seed green for verified/live states.
- Frame 10 is clearly editorial motion graphics, never simulated application UI.

## Media treatment

- Preserve the recordings' native color and legibility.
- Add a very light warm vignette only where an overlay needs separation.
- No aggressive grading, blur, artificial depth of field, or fake cursor clicks.
